What I help with

The everyday things. Nothing is too small to ask.

  • iPhone & AndroidText too small, ringer off, storage full, mystery pop‑ups.
  • PhotosSending pictures to the grandkids, finding old ones, making room.
  • FaceTime & ZoomAnswering a video call without hanging up. Joining the family call.
  • Wi‑Fi & printerBack online, printer connected, the TV app that quit.
  • PasswordsBack into email or Apple ID, and a simple way to keep them that you control.
  • ScamsFake calls, texts and pop‑ups. We stop, look, and check together.

Not me: cracked screens, data recovery, anything to do with your bank or money apps, caregiving. I teach — I don’t take over.
