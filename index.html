What I help with

The everyday things that quietly drive people nuts.

  • 📱iPhone & AndroidToo‑small text, ringer off, storage full, “what is this pop‑up?”
  • 🖼️PhotosTexting pictures to the grandkids, finding old photos, freeing up space.
  • 📹FaceTime & ZoomAnswering a video call without hanging up. Joining the family call.
  • 📶Wi‑Fi & printerGetting back online, connecting the printer, the TV app that stopped working.
  • 🔑PasswordsGetting back into email or Apple ID, and a simple way to keep passwords you control.
  • 🛑Scams“Is this real?” Fake calls, texts and pop‑ups. We stop, look, and check together.

Not me: cracked screens or hardware repair, data recovery, anything to do with your bank or money apps, or caregiving. I teach; I don’t take over.

If your problem is bigger than the table, I’ll say so, show you the rate card, and we can set up a home visit. No pressure either way.

Questions people ask

Do I need to bring anything to the table?
Just the device. If you have passwords written somewhere, bring those too — it saves time.
What if you can’t fix it?
At the table, nothing. On a home visit, you pay $40 instead of the full rate, and I’ll tell you who can help.
Can my daughter pay?
Yes. She can book by email and pay online. She can also be on speaker during the visit.
Do you fix cracked screens or recover lost files?
No. That’s a repair shop. I help you use what you have.
Are you staying in the area?
I’m here for the season, then moving to communities in the Coachella Valley. Anyone who wants to keep going can, by phone or video, for $30 a call or $30 a month.
Is this like a “tech support” phone number?
No. I’m one person you’ll meet in your clubhouse. I will never call you out of the blue, ask you to buy anything, or ask for a bank login. If someone does, that’s a scam — hang up.
