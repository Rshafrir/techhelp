TechHelp

Joshua Tree and Yucca Valley

Help with your phone, tablet, or computer.

I’m Ron. I give in-person tech help to people who live in 55+ communities. I show up where you already gather and help with ordinary problems: phones, Zoom and FaceTime, photos, Wi‑Fi, passwords, and spotting scams.

The free session in the clubhouse is the Tech Table. If you want more than that, you can book a home visit or a phone or video call.

Email hello@techhelp.now

Who pays

The community pays nothing. It provides a room and lets residents know the table is there.

You pay only when you want more than the free session.

Prices

Each hour after that $70
Same-day home visit $25 extra
Home visit that cannot be solved $40, not the full visit
Phone or video call $30
Monthly plan $30 a month for 3 calls. Extra calls are $30 each.

Payment

Venmo or Zelle in person. Card online through Stripe.

How to book

Email hello@techhelp.now. Say where you live and what you need help with. I will write back to set a time.

For the activity director

The Tech Table is free for the community. The community pays nothing. I need a room, and a way for residents to know the table is there.

Residents pay only when they want more than the free session. They book that with me directly, at the prices above, so it does not become work for your staff.

Email me about hosting a Tech Table

What I don’t do

  • I don’t repair cracked screens or recover lost files.
  • I don’t log in to bank accounts or money apps.
  • I don’t keep your passwords.
  • I don’t provide caregiving.
  • I don’t call you out of the blue. If someone calls, says they are TechHelp, and asks for money or a password, hang up.

Where I’ll be

Joshua Tree and Yucca Valley for this season. After that, 55+ communities in the Coachella Valley. Help by phone or video continues after I leave an area.
