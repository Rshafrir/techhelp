A Tech Table in your clubhouse

Residents bring a phone, tablet, or computer and a question, and Ron helps them sort it out on the spot. Anything that takes about 10 minutes at the table is free.

How it starts

  1. Email Ron

    Tell him a little about your clubhouse and the best way to reach you.

  2. Settle a day and time

    Ron writes back to arrange when the table will be there.

  3. Post the flyer

    Fill in the day and time, print it, and put it where residents will see it.

At the table

Each resident gets about 10 minutes with their own device. These are the usual topics.

  • Phones and tablets
  • Zoom and FaceTime
  • Photos
  • Wi‑Fi
  • Passwords
  • Spotting scams

When it needs longer

Ron offers a call or a home visit, which the resident sets up with him.

  • Calls
  • Phone or video call $30
  • Monthly plan, 3 calls $30 a month
  • Home visits
  • First hour $100
  • Each hour after that $70
  • Same day $25 extra
  • If it can’t be solved $40, not the full visit

Things to print

  • Flyer For the bulletin board. Type in the day and time, then print.
  • Table card Fold it in half and stand it on the table.
  • Price list A handout for residents who want to keep one.
