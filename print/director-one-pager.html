TechHelp

Phone, tablet, and computer help · Joshua Tree & Yucca Valley

A free weekly Tech Table for your residents. No cost to the community.

I’m Ron. For two hours a week, at the same day and time, I sit in your clubhouse or activity room. Residents bring a phone, tablet, or laptop. I help with texting, photos, FaceTime, Wi‑Fi, passwords, and scam calls. About 10 minutes per person is free. If someone needs more time, they book a home visit with me at a posted price, so it does not become work for your staff.

What you provide

  • A table and two chairs where people already walk by
  • A line in the newsletter or on the bulletin board
  • Your vendor paperwork — I’ll complete whatever you use (insurance, background check, W‑9)

What residents get

  • About ten minutes of free, patient help per visit
  • A written note of anything we changed
  • Scam awareness, one tip at a time
  • Family can sit in or listen by phone

When a resident needs more than ten minutes

They book directly with me at posted rates, so it never lands on your team: home visit $100 first hour, $70 each hour after; phone or video help $30 a call or $30/month for three. Adult children can book and pay remotely for a parent. When my season here ends, residents who want to keep going can, by phone or video.

Boundaries, so you know

I don’t touch residents’ bank or money accounts, I don’t keep their passwords, and I don’t do caregiving, repairs or anything medical. I only see residents at the table or by appointment — never door to door. If anyone appears unable to consent to help, I stop and involve family.

Proposed pilot

Four weeks. Day from time to time, starting date, in room. If residents show up, we keep going.
