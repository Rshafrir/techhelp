TechHelp

Phone, tablet, and computer help · Joshua Tree & Yucca Valley

A free Tech Table. No cost to the community.

I’m Ron. I help residents with phones, Zoom and FaceTime, photos, Wi‑Fi, passwords, and spotting scams. The free session is the Tech Table: about 10 minutes in the clubhouse.

What the community provides

  • A room
  • A way for residents to know the table is there

The community pays nothing.

What a resident gets

  • About 10 minutes of free help at the table
  • A home visit or a phone or video call when they want more time

The resident pays only for more than the free session, and books that with me.

When a resident wants more than 10 minutes

They book with me directly, so it does not become work for your staff. Home visit $100 for the first hour, $70 each hour after that. Same-day home visit $25 extra. A home visit that cannot be solved is $40, not the full visit. A phone or video call is $30. A monthly plan is $30 a month for 3 calls; extra calls are $30 each.

Anything that can be finished in about 10 minutes at the table is free. Anything longer is paid. Venmo or Zelle in person. Card online through Stripe.

What I don’t do

I don’t repair cracked screens or recover lost files. I don’t log in to bank accounts or money apps. I don’t keep passwords. I don’t provide caregiving.

Proposed time

Day at time, in room.
